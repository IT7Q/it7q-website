About IT7Q

We make enterprise-grade technology simple, safe and affordable for Queensland homes, community groups and small businesses.

Our story

IT7Q Cyber-Tech Services started in 2020 in Kallangur, Queensland. It's Australian owned and operated, and it grew from one simple idea: everyday people deserve the same protection and capability as big business, without the corporate price tag or the jargon.

IT7Q was founded in 2020 by Chris Lord, an eighth-generation Australian based in Kallangur, Queensland. Married with four kids, Chris knows first-hand what home users and small businesses need from their tech. With a diploma in IT Systems Administration and years of hands-on support, he built IT7Q around one idea: serious security, explained in plain English, at a price small operators and home users can afford. Today IT7Q spans seven divisions, from managed IT and our own software to handmade woodcraft, and everything we build, we build in-house.
