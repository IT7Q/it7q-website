AI Automations

Real automations we've built — and how they can save your business hours every week

🔄

Automated Patch Management

Save 6–8 hours per week

Our AI agents scan all managed clients daily at 6AM, vet every Windows update against known vulnerability databases, score risk levels automatically, and only alert a human when something genuinely needs attention. Approved patches deploy themselves.

🏢 Perfect for SMBs with multiple computers
📱

Social Media Content Pipeline

Save 4–6 hours per week

Agents scrape weekly specials from suppliers, automatically write platform-specific ad copy (different tone for Facebook vs LinkedIn vs Instagram), queue drafts for your review, and post once approved. No copywriter needed.

🛒 Perfect for retail and trade businesses
📋

Invoice & PABX Processing

Save 3–4 hours per month

Automatically parse phone company invoices, apply client-specific markup rules, calculate GST, and generate ready-to-send invoices in seconds. What used to take an hour per client now takes seconds per batch.

📞 Perfect for businesses with large phone bills
📧

Email Triage & Response

Save 2–3 hours per day

AI reads incoming enquiries, categorises urgency, drafts responses in your business's tone, and routes to the right person. High-priority items get immediate alerts. Routine enquiries get professional auto-responses while you focus on real work.

📨 Perfect for service businesses with high email volume
🔒

Security Monitoring & Alerting

24/7 protection, zero manual effort

Continuous monitoring of login attempts, network anomalies, and threat indicators across all managed systems. Automatic blocking of known bad actors via CrowdSec, with human-readable daily reports every morning.

🛡️ Perfect for any business with internet-connected systems
📊

Compliance & Reporting

Never miss a deadline again

Automated monthly compliance reports, backup verification, service level agreement tracking, and client digest emails — all generated and delivered without lifting a finger. Board reports, incident summaries, health dashboards — automatic.

🏛️ Perfect for not-for-profits and community organisations
